Our journey

Valentina & Carlotta

A company built to put students first.

Our journey began in 2018, when Valentina and Carlotta met at university. They were both students with big dreams, ambitious goals, and a shared passion for helping others succeed. As their friendship grew, so did their vision.

They worked together throughout their careers in higher education and student recruitment, gaining valuable experience while always holding onto one dream: to build a company that puts students first. That dream became VM Education Recruitment.
